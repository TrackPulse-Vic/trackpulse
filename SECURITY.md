# Security Policy
We take the security of TrackPulse seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please do not report it via public GitHub issues. Instead, please follow the process below:

## How to Report
Please send an email to [xm9g@outlook.com](mailto:xm9g@outlook.com) with the following details:
- Description: A detailed summary of the vulnerability.
- Impact: What could an attacker achieve if this is exploited?
- Steps to Reproduce: A clear list of steps (or a proof-of-concept script) to demonstrate the issue.

## What to Expect
Once you submit a report, you can expect the following:
- Acknowledgment: You will receive a response within 24-48 hours confirming we have received the report.
- Investigation: Our team will validate the finding and determine its severity. We may reach out for further clarification.
- Status Updates: We will provide an update at least once a week until the issue is resolved.
- Resolution: If the vulnerability is accepted, we will work on a fix and coordinate a public disclosure date with you.

### Disclosure Policy
We ask that you follow responsible disclosure practices—giving us a reasonable amount of time to fix the issue before sharing any information publicly. In return, we will provide credit for your discovery in our repo (unless you prefer to remain anonymous).
